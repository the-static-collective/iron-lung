## 1. Purpose

The recovered Recursive T5 Combinatrix design and the newer Sigil Mathematics work now appear to be describing neighboring parts of the same deeper machine.

The earlier design already established:

```text
Braid
├── S — Substance
├── L — Lineage
└── A — Authority
```

and the first deliberately naive recursive expression:

```text
X[n+1] = A(L(S(X[n])))
```

It immediately rejected that expression as constitutionally incomplete because Authority cannot be treated as an ordinary generative transform.

The safer architecture became:

```text
constituted braid B[n]
        ↓
S — bounded substance possibility
        ↓
L — continuity / ancestry pressure
        ↓
A — authority requirement surface
        ↓
bounded possibility field P[n]
        ↓
PRESENT ADMISSION
        ↓
HEART BEAT
        ↓
B[n+1] + receipt
        ↺
```

The new clue is that this machine may support two unlike representational projections at every layer:

```text
verbal
mathal
```

The hypothesis is **not** that words should be translated directly into equations, or that mathematics should be paraphrased back into prose until the two agree.

The stronger hypothesis is:

> **Verbal and mathal expressions are distinct, lossy, inspectable projections of a deeper relational object. Recursive circulation between those projections may expose invariants, contradictions, gaps, topology, and residue that neither projection reveals alone.**

Iron Lung supplies the constitutional boundary that keeps such recursive mutual pressure from becoming counterfeit history.

This document therefore extends PR #3 with a design for:

1. a deeper relational core;
2. verbal and mathal projections of that core;
3. a typed Connector that witnesses crossings without claiming identity;
4. a Lung operator that recursively circulates through possibility-space;
5. a Sigil Algebra that can provide explicit spatial/transformation operators;
6. Addressable Absence as a first-class typed wound rather than null;
7. a Heart operator that alone may constitute an externally admitted transition into history.

## 2. What the recovered design already proves

The Recursive T5 design should not be re-described as "three T5s in sequence."

Its deeper object is already the **Combinatrix**.

The naive image was:

```text
C = S × L × A
```

but the design explicitly rejected materializing the full Cartesian product. The useful object is a bounded compatibility / constraint surface.

Let:

- `S` be bounded Substance candidates;
- `L` be bounded Lineage postures / consequences;
- `A` be bounded Authority requirement postures;
- `K_n(s,l,a)` be the local compatibility predicate under the present historical cut.

Then define:

\[
\mathcal{C}_n = \{(s,l,a) \in S \times L \times A \mid K_n(s,l,a)=1\}
\]

`C_n` is not "the answer."

It is the currently reachable constrained possibility surface.

Core inherited law:

> **Constraint may narrow possibility-space. Narrowing must not counterfeit selection.**

A candidate may disappear because it violates an attributable invariant. It may not disappear merely because another candidate ranks higher.

A surviving candidate does not become history merely because it survived constraint.

## 3. The constitutional distinction: possibility is not history

This is the load-bearing distinction of the whole machine.

Define a Lung/proposal operator:

\[
\Phi : B \rightarrow \mathcal{P}
\]

where `B` is a constituted braid and `P` is a bounded proposal / possibility field.

Then recursive breaths are meaningful:

\[
\Phi^0(B), \Phi^1(B), \Phi^2(B), \Phi^3(B), \ldots
\]

But none of the following imply a constituted transition:

```text
candidate generated
candidate repeated
candidate survives constraint
candidate converges
candidate is rank 1
candidate appears in many recursive breaths
candidate is mutually supported by verbal and mathal projections
```

Constitution requires a different kind of edge.

Define Heart as a **partial admitted transition**:

\[
H(B_n, p, w) \rightharpoonup (B_{n+1}, r)
\]

where:

- `p` is a candidate from a bounded proposal field;
- `w` is the required present warrant / witness / selection supplied from outside recursive generation;
- `r` is the terminal receipt;
- `H` may be undefined or refuse.

Therefore:

\[
p \in \Phi^k(B_n) \not\Rightarrow B_n \rightarrow B_{n+1}
\]

Compression:

> **Reachable in possibility is not entered into history.**

And the inherited Heart/Lung law remains:

> **The Lung may breathe through possibilities. The Heart beats only when consequence becomes present history.**

## 4. Relational core before representation

The new design should not make verbal and mathal representations translate directly into one another as though one were canonical and the other derivative.

Introduce a deeper relational object:

\[
R
\]

`R` is not initially required to be one universal schema. It is a local structured relation sufficient for the present specimen.

It may contain relations such as:

```text
identity / distinction
part / whole
inside / outside
adjacency
connection
direction
order
constraint
gap
transformation
lineage edge
authority requirement
```

The important rule is that representation is a projection of `R`, not `R` itself.

Define:

\[
\pi_V(R) = V
\]

for verbal projection, and:

\[
\pi_M(R) = M
\]

for mathal projection.

Define bounded interpretation functions:

\[
\iota_V(V) \rightarrow R_V
\]

\[
\iota_M(M) \rightarrow R_M
\]

These are explicitly not guaranteed inverses.

In general:

\[
\iota_V(\pi_V(R)) \neq R
\]

and:

\[
\iota_M(\pi_M(R)) \neq R
\]

That inequality is not automatically an error.

It creates **representation residue**.

## 5. Verbal and mathal are unlike witness surfaces

### 5.1 Verbal projection

The verbal surface is good at preserving and exposing things such as:

- ambiguity;
- analogy;
- intent;
- counterfactual phrasing;
- descriptive distinction;
- causal stories;
- human-readable contradiction;
- contextual qualification;
- unresolved meaning.

It is also capable of hiding structural contradiction beneath fluent prose.

### 5.2 Mathal projection

`Mathal` is a provisional name for an explicit relational / mathematical representation that is neither ordinary prose nor an opaque model embedding.

It may include:

- operators;
- typed relations;
- variables;
- equations;
- inequalities;
- topology;
- graph edges;
- constraint sets;
- transformations;
- state-transition relations;
- gaps / sentinels;
- receipts or lineage references where mathematically relevant.

It is good at exposing things such as:

- contradiction;
- impossible constraint combinations;
- invariance;
- symmetry;
- order sensitivity;
- reachability;
- cycle structure;
- fixed points;
- conservation-like constraints;
- explicit unknowns.

It is also capable of hiding semantic ambiguity by giving an underspecified relation a falsely precise notation.

### 5.3 Neither projection outranks the other by representation type alone

Core law:

> **Formal notation is not automatically more truthful than language, and fluent language is not automatically more meaningful than formal notation. Each projection is a fallible witness surface.**

A mathal expression does not gain authority merely because it is formal.

A verbal expression does not gain substance merely because it is persuasive.

## 6. Connector — witnessed crossing, not equivalence

The **Connector** is the typed membrane between representational worlds.

It is not simply a translator.

A Connector records:

```text
source representation
source relation refs
projection / interpretation rule
result representation
derived relation refs
residue / unresolved mismatch
provenance
confidence or bounded uncertainty where applicable
authority: none unless separately supplied
```

A Connector therefore makes a claim of the form:

> **Under this declared projection or interpretation rule, this output is presently related to that input in this way.**

It does not make the stronger claim:

> **These two representations are identical.**

Compression:

```text
projection != identity
translation != equivalence
agreement != authority
```

### 6.1 Residue is first-class

For a verbal round trip:

```text
R
 ↓ pi_V
V
 ↓ iota_V
R_V
```

retain:

\[
\Delta_V = \operatorname{diff}(R, R_V)
\]

Likewise:

\[
\Delta_M = \operatorname{diff}(R, R_M)
\]

The implementation need not immediately define one universal numeric distance metric.

The first law is weaker and more important:

> **Do not silently discard representational difference.**

Residue may contain exactly the clue the other representation failed to preserve.

## 10. Addressable Absence as a mathematical object

The recovered primitive remains one of the strongest pieces of the system:

> **Do not erase the hole. Give it an address.**

A gap is not `null`.

Let:

\[
g_S, g_L, g_A
\]

represent typed absences in Substance, Lineage, and Authority.

Each gap may have:

```text
gapRef
type
historical cut
boundary / location
creation event
known surrounding relations
candidate fillings or transformations
closure requirements
current state
```

The gap asserts that something relevant is absent or unresolved at an address.

It does not assert that a lawful solution exists.

### 10.1 CUT may produce a gap rather than nothing

A provisional Sigil Algebra interpretation:

\[
\operatorname{CUT}(X) \rightarrow (X', g_i)
\]

where `g_i` remains addressable.

A later operation may propose:

\[
\operatorname{FILL}(g_i, x) \rightarrow X''
\]

but history must preserve:

```text
gap existed
candidate x was later proposed
closure occurred or did not occur under an attributable event
```

The ancestor must never be rewritten as though `x` had always occupied the gap.

### 10.2 Do not call the gap zero yet

Addressable Absence may eventually have algebraic relations to zero-like objects, identity elements, null spaces, holes, boundaries, or complements.

Do not force that equivalence early.

Zero carries existing algebraic obligations.

A typed historical wound is already useful without pretending it satisfies them.

## 13. Fixed points, cycles, and resonant orbits

The recovered design explicitly anticipated fixed points and oscillation.

That becomes stronger under verbal/mathal circulation.

### 13.1 Fixed point

\[
\Psi(R)=R
\]

means only:

> **stable under this declared operator and present cut.**

It does not mean true, globally complete, optimal, or authorized.

### 13.2 Periodic orbit

A period-2 orbit:

\[
\Psi^2(R)=R, \quad \Psi(R)\neq R
\]

may expose a real unresolved incompatibility.

Do not collapse:

```text
A -> B -> A -> B
```

into whichever state happened to be most recent.

### 13.3 Cross-representational partial convergence

A particularly useful future witness is asymmetric stability.

For example:

\[
M_{n+1}=M_n
\]

while:

\[
V_{n+1}\neq V_n
\]

The mathal description may have stabilized while the verbal surface continues exposing distinctions.

Or the reverse.

That disagreement should be preserved as evidence about representational limits.

### 13.4 Resonant orbit rather than forced fixed point

Some systems may never collapse to one representation-stable point.

A bounded recurring orbit may be the right local terminal description.

The architecture should be able to say:

```text
stable cycle
period: k
verbal residue: bounded
mathal residue: bounded
unresolved relation: retained
heart beats: 0
```

rather than forcing consensus.

## 15. Generative ancestry and perceptual decomposition are different histories

A generated object should preserve at least two distinguishable structures:

```text
GENERATIVE ANCESTRY
what operands and operators actually produced the object

CURRENT PERCEPTUAL DECOMPOSITION
what parts, gaps, symmetries, or substructures are presently recognized in it
```

The first is historical and immutable.

The second may be plural, local, revisable, and representation-dependent.

Therefore:

```text
how it was made
!=
what can now be seen in it
```

A later perception may become computationally consequential only through an explicit adoption event.

That event joins history without rewriting history.

## 17. Authority remains deliberately asymmetric

Authority is not another generative medium.

A verbal system may phrase a required approval.

A mathal system may express a warrant requirement as a constraint.

A Sigil system may depict a closed gate or unresolved edge.

None of those may create the authority they describe.

For an authority gap `g_A`, generative systems may produce:

```text
required witness: human approval
required capability: cap:X
required receipt: R
status: unresolved
```

They may not produce:

```text
approval probably intended
        ↓
authorized
```

Core law from the recovered design remains mandatory:

> **An authority gap may generate a question, requirement, or proposal envelope. It may not generate the authority that closes itself.**

The Heart must therefore require the present admission input from outside recursive generation.
