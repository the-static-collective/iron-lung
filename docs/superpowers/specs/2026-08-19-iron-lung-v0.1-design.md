# Iron Lung v0.1 — Own-Birth Braided Circulation Design

**Status:** approved in conversation; written design awaiting human review before implementation planning

**Canonical project issue:** #1

## 1. Purpose

Iron Lung is a boundary-and-transformation instrument. Its first specimen is its own birth.

The v0.1 problem is not to simulate thermodynamics, prove a theology, or build a generic service mesh. It is to make one small executable model of how a system can receive something from outside itself, preserve enough self-knowledge during movement, route damage toward relevant repair capability, and become a new constituted present without allowing proposal, metaphor, or future preference to impersonate evidence or present authority.

Working question:

> **What crossed the boundary that made this transformation possible, what remained continuous while it moved, and by what authority did the crossing occur?**

Iron Lung treats the answer as a three-stranded braid.

## 2. Two-layer lung

Iron Lung carries two explicitly separated descriptive layers.

### 2.1 Literal layer

The literal layer may describe only project-native, inspectable facts or explicit declared inputs such as:

- what substance/reference reached a boundary;
- what lineage/provenance references accompany it;
- what authority/warrant or refusal accompanies it;
- which capability registrations are currently admitted;
- which routing options are structurally available;
- which route, if any, present authority selected;
- which repair produced a descendant braid;
- which witnessed result became part of the next constituted cut.

The literal layer must not infer physical energy, entropy, thermodynamic work, spiritual action, or metaphysical causation from software events.

### 2.2 Pneuma layer

The pneuma layer is an explicitly interpretive hypothesis space for ideas such as breath, spirit, received possibility, encounter, and newness.

It may:

- annotate a transformation with an interpretive question or hypothesis;
- influence which prospective questions are presented to a human;
- provide language for describing the liminal interval between settled states.

It may not:

- become evidence merely because it is recorded;
- authorize a transition;
- impersonate physical measurement;
- rewrite the literal layer;
- claim a proposed future is already constituted.

The boundary between the two layers is part of the design, not a temporary implementation detail.

## 3. Braided Transfer invariant

Every transferable object is represented by exactly three strands:

1. **Substance** — what is being carried.
2. **Lineage** — what the carried thing remains continuous with.
3. **Authority** — what crossing, handling, or transformation is permitted and witnessed.

A transfer is never one- or two-stranded. Missing knowledge is represented explicitly rather than by dropping a strand.

Conceptually:

```text
Braid
├── substance
├── lineage
└── authority
```

The braid preserves three irreducible questions:

```text
What is this?
What is it continuous with?
By what authority may it move or change?
```

The three strands distribute tension. No aggregate confidence or health score may erase disagreement among them.

### 3.1 Strand state

Each strand carries an explicit condition. The initial vocabulary is deliberately small:

```text
intact | strained | unknown | refused | broken
```

`none` may be used only as an explicit strand value where the domain truth is that no substance, lineage claim, or authority is being asserted. It is not equivalent to omission.

A braid therefore always preserves the fact that a question exists even when its answer is missing, damaged, or refused.

### 3.2 Immutable cuts

A braid is an immutable historical cut. Repair does not mutate an old braid.

Any repair or transformation produces a descendant braid that references its predecessor and the witness or admitted act that accounts for the change.

```text
Braid_n
   ↓ admitted bounded transformation
Braid_n+1
```

The old damaged braid remains part of history.

## 4. Circulation is not assimilation

Iron Lung distinguishes three states that must not collapse:

```text
transport != admission != assimilation
```

- **Transport:** the braid reached or moved through a boundary/circulatory surface.
- **Admission:** a present authority permitted a bounded handling or transformation.
- **Assimilation:** the resulting braid is allowed to participate in the next constituted self.

A braid may be circulable while non-admissible or non-assimilable.

Example:

```text
substance: intact
lineage: broken
authority: refused
```

Iron Lung may truthfully record that such a braid reached the organism and continue routing it for inspection without claiming that it became part of the organism.

Core law:

> **Anything may be witnessed at the boundary. Only an appropriately admitted braid may be assimilated into the next self.**

## 5. Capability-based circulation

Iron Lung does not use a central router with hard-coded knowledge of every organ.

Instead, organs publish bounded capability registrations describing which strand states they can inspect or repair and what prerequisites apply.

Conceptually:

```text
CapabilityRegistration
- capabilityId
- organId
- accepts
- requires
- mayProduce
- authorityRef
- witnessRefs
```

A capability registration is not trusted merely because an organ advertises it. The registration itself must be admitted/witnessed before circulation may consider it routable.

Examples:

```text
accepts: lineage = broken
mayProduce: lineage = strained | intact
requires: substance != unknown
```

or:

```text
accepts: authority = refused
mayProduce: authority = refused | strained | intact
requires: no authority escalation
```

The vascular layer may match braid state to admitted capability. It must not perform the repair itself.

Core law:

> **The vascular system may route toward repair; it may not manufacture repair.**

## 6. Repair manifold

Given a braid `B` and the organism's currently admitted capability registrations, Iron Lung computes the bounded set of locally reachable repair options.

Conceptually:

```text
RepairManifold(B) = { admitted capabilities that can lawfully accept B }
```

This is a proposal-space calculation, not execution.

A single braid may expose:

- no lawful route;
- exactly one lawful route;
- multiple simultaneously lawful routes.

No-route is a valid result. Iron Lung must not manufacture a path merely to make progress.

When a repair organ acts, it emits a descendant braid. The circulation layer then evaluates that new braid from scratch rather than following a precomputed global pipeline.

This creates a state-dependent repair topology:

```text
B0 -> organ A -> B1 -> organ C -> B2
```

The next available doors are a function of the current braid and admitted capabilities, not a centrally authored repair itinerary.

## 7. Forks ascend the Continuity Spine

When more than one repair route is simultaneously lawful, Iron Lung must not silently select among them.

The braid plus bounded route set ascends to the **Continuity Spine** for prospective evaluation.

The Spine may consider:

- origin/lineage;
- constituted present;
- invariants that must remain carried;
- proposed attractor;
- transfer/witness requirements;
- consequences each prospective route would require or shed.

The Spine may reject structurally unlawful candidates and rank or annotate the remaining lawful routes.

It may not select or authorize a route.

Core law inherited from Continuity Spine:

> **The future may constrain proposals backward; it may not grant evidence backward.**

The route choice remains an act of admitted present authority.

### 7.1 Directional distinction

Iron Lung keeps ascending and descending flow distinct:

```text
ascending: evidence/results -> update the self-model
descending: constraints/proposals -> shape possible becoming
```

Descending flow cannot constitute the future. Ascending flow cannot silently become permission.

## 8. Nerves and veins remain orthogonal

A nervous signal may request, observe, or react to circulation. It cannot impersonate the thing being carried.

Core law:

> **Signals may request circulation; they may not impersonate circulation.**

The separation is intentional:

- **Nerves:** event/signal/request/attention.
- **Veins:** carried braid.
- **Organs:** bounded transformations.
- **Continuity Spine:** temporal self-model and prospective route evaluation.
- **Present authority:** admission/selection.

This prevents control messages from silently acquiring substance, provenance, or authority simply because they influenced routing.

## 9. Own-birth specimen

Iron Lung v0.1 proves exactly one local specimen: the lawful path by which Iron Lung becomes capable of recording braided transformation paths.

The initial birth braid is intentionally incomplete but always three-stranded.

Its conceptual starting state is:

```text
substance:
  the newborn repository plus the proposed project artifacts

lineage:
  the originating conversation and project-backed Continuity Spine context

authority:
  explicit human approval to create this project and slice this design
```

The executable specimen must preserve a distinction between references to external evidence and locally constituted facts. It must not claim access to conversation contents, Continuity Spine internals, or human intent that is not represented by explicit supplied references/witness inputs.

The first-breath path should demonstrate at least:

1. creation of a valid three-strand birth braid;
2. an explicit strain or incompleteness that leaves the braid circulable but not yet assimilable;
3. discovery of one or more admitted local repair capabilities;
4. production of a bounded repair manifold;
5. a fork, if multiple lawful routes are part of the fixture, escalated to a Continuity Spine proposal boundary rather than auto-selected;
6. explicit present-authority route selection supplied from outside the evaluator;
7. a repair that emits a descendant braid rather than mutating the original;
8. re-evaluation of the descendant braid;
9. a terminal first-breath result that can distinguish `circulated`, `blocked`, `awaiting_selection`, and `assimilable` states without self-certifying evidence.

The project's first executable act therefore records the path by which it acquires the capability to record paths.

That self-reference is allowed only because the bootstrap remains visible and externally witnessed inputs remain externally supplied.

## 10. Proposed v0.1 components

The design favors small pure units rather than an orchestrator.

### 10.1 Braid validator

Responsibilities:

- require exactly the three named strands;
- validate explicit strand conditions;
- validate parent/descendant references;
- reject omitted or ambiguous strands;
- return normalized immutable data.

It does not determine truth of supplied provenance or authority claims.

### 10.2 Capability validator

Responsibilities:

- validate bounded accepted strand conditions;
- validate declared prerequisites and possible output conditions;
- require explicit authority/witness references for registration;
- reject registrations that claim unrestricted repair scope.

It does not execute a repair.

### 10.3 Circulation evaluator

Responsibilities:

- accept one braid plus admitted capability registrations;
- compute matching locally routable capabilities;
- preserve per-strand damage information;
- return no-route / one-route / multi-route state;
- never choose among multiple lawful routes.

### 10.4 Prospective fork boundary

Responsibilities:

- package a braid and its multi-route repair manifold for Continuity Spine evaluation;
- accept Spine annotations/ranking as proposal-only input;
- require a separate present-authority selection to continue.

Iron Lung v0.1 should model this boundary locally rather than adding a live cross-repository dependency on TranchNode.

### 10.5 Repair application

Responsibilities:

- accept a selected admitted capability plus explicit repair result/witness input;
- produce a descendant braid;
- preserve unchanged strand identities where applicable;
- preserve the damaged ancestor;
- reject repairs that silently widen authority or erase lineage.

The repair application records supplied results; it does not autonomously perform domain-specific repairs.

### 10.6 Assimilation evaluator

Responsibilities:

- decide whether the current braid is structurally eligible for assimilation under declared local policy;
- distinguish circulability from assimilation eligibility;
- fail closed on missing required witnesses, unresolved authority, or broken required lineage.

It does not promote a braid into external ecosystem authority.

## 12. Error and refusal semantics

Iron Lung should use explicit refusal/finding codes rather than exceptions for expected domain outcomes.

Initial conceptual findings include:

```text
invalid_braid
missing_strand
invalid_strand_state
unadmitted_capability
no_repair_route
multiple_routes_require_selection
unknown_route_selection
repair_scope_violation
authority_escalation
lineage_erasure
ancestor_mutation
assimilation_blocked
proposal_only
```

Malformed API/programmer input may still be represented as validation failure, but domain refusal is a first-class result.

No error path may silently delete the braid that triggered it.

## 13. Determinism and mutation rules

For identical normalized inputs, all pure evaluators must produce byte-for-byte stable logical output independent of input array ordering where order is not semantically meaningful.

No evaluator may mutate:

- the supplied braid;
- ancestor braids;
- capability registrations;
- witness inputs;
- prospective route annotations.

Repair creates new data. Evaluation does not alter history.

## 14. Testing strategy

Implementation planning should preserve a strict test-first sequence.

The minimum test matrix should cover:

- rejection when any of the three strands is omitted;
- acceptance of explicit `unknown` / `refused` / `broken` states without dropping the strand;
- damaged braid remains circulable;
- broken lineage routes only to admitted matching capabilities;
- unadmitted self-declared organ capability is ignored/refused;
- no matching capability returns a stable no-route result;
- multiple lawful capabilities return the full bounded set and do not auto-select;
- Spine ranking/annotation cannot become selection authority;
- present selection must name one offered route;
- repair emits a descendant and leaves ancestor unchanged;
- repair cannot silently widen authority;
- repair cannot erase required lineage;
- descendant is re-evaluated rather than inheriting stale route assumptions;
- transport/admission/assimilation remain distinct;
- pneuma annotation cannot affect literal evidence/authority fields;
- repeated evaluation is deterministic and non-mutating;
- own-birth fixture reaches a first-breath terminal result only with explicitly supplied witnesses/selection.

## 15. Non-goals

Iron Lung v0.1 does not provide:

- a thermodynamic simulator;
- physical energy or entropy accounting;
- theology presented as measurement;
- proof or disproof of Spirit;
- autonomous repair execution;
- autonomous route selection;
- a generic service mesh or message broker;
- network transport;
- multi-repository mutation;
- direct runtime dependency on TranchNode;
- a universal vascular schema for the ecosystem;
- shared cross-project authority;
- deletion or mutation of historical braids;
- a general workflow engine;
- a scheduler;
- an AI planner.

## 16. Relationship to Continuity Spine

Continuity Spine models lawful transformation through time:

```text
inherit -> grow -> overlap -> transfer -> witness -> shed -> continue
```

Iron Lung models what crosses a boundary during such becoming and how damaged transfers remain self-describing enough to reach relevant repair capability.

The two are complementary:

```text
Continuity Spine: how the self may become through time
Iron Lung:       what crosses the membrane while becoming is possible
```

Iron Lung may send a bounded multi-route proposal upward to a Spine-shaped evaluator. It does not copy TranchNode's local schema authority into this repository.

The first implementation should therefore use a local minimal prospective-boundary contract sufficient to prove the relationship without claiming schema convergence.

## 17. Graduation boundary

This first specimen is local evidence only.

Do not promote `Braided Transfer`, `Repair Manifold`, or this exact schema into a shared ecosystem Pattern solely because Iron Lung can prove its own birth.

A materially different second system should reproduce the deeper invariant locally before cross-project graduation:

> **Movement preserves three distinct questions: what crossed, what it remains continuous with, and by what authority it may move or change. Damage to one answer must not erase the other two.**

If a second system proves a different local shape with the same invariant, a later BEE-style boundary transfer may carry the invariant while leaving implementation authority behind.

## 18. Design compression

Iron Lung v0.1 is governed by these laws:

1. **Always three strands.** Substance, lineage, authority remain distinct and explicit.
2. **Damage remains visible.** Strained braids may circulate; they are not silently assimilated.
3. **Repair is capability-based.** Circulation discovers bounded admitted organs; it does not repair.
4. **Repair creates descendants.** History is not rewritten.
5. **Forks ascend the Spine.** Multiple lawful routes become proposal space, not automatic choice.
6. **Future constrains; present authorizes.** The Spine may rank, never select.
7. **Nerves do not become veins.** Signals may request circulation; they do not impersonate carried state.
8. **Literal and pneuma layers stay separate.** Metaphor can generate questions without impersonating evidence.

Operationally:

```text
inhale -> braid -> inspect -> route -> repair -> re-evaluate -> admit -> assimilate -> exhale
```

with the crucial branch:

```text
multiple lawful routes -> ascend Spine -> proposal/ranking -> present selection -> continue
```

The first breath is successful when Iron Lung can truthfully account for the path by which its own birth braid became structurally assimilable without self-certifying the evidence or authority that made the birth possible.
